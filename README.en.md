### InsightFlow AI · Turn fragmented consumer signals into verifiable decisions

In cross-border operations, the hard part is rarely a lack of information. The problem is that useful signals are scattered across product pages, reviews, videos, search trends and competitor evidence — making research slow and decisions easy to fall back on intuition.

**InsightFlow AI** turns those signals into structured consumer insight, then connects them to product opportunities, content direction and GTM actions.

- Multi-source evidence: Google Shopping, Walmart, YouTube, Google Trends and public community discussions
- Evidence-first: original sources stay visible; an LLM summary is never treated as ground truth
- Two action layers: **product / supply actions** and **content / GTM actions**
- Cases: Magnetic Power Bank · US/AU; Insta360 X6 Launch Intelligence

👉 [View project](https://github.com/Meriky323/insightflow-ai)

### Link Checker · Automating pre-launch QA for marketing links

Before an EDM campaign goes live, UTM parameters, discount codes, redirects and destination pages often need repetitive manual checks. It is tedious work, but a small mistake can directly hurt campaign conversion.

I turned that workflow into a lightweight web tool:

- checks UTM / campaign parameters
- validates discount-code and redirect paths
- detects nested links, short links and destination domains
- supports optional real-browser verification
- produces structured QA results before launch

👉 [View project](https://github.com/Meriky323/link-checker)

## Questions I care about

**Why do consumers actually buy — or decide not to?**  
Purchase drivers, barriers, contexts and trade-offs are more useful than a simple positive/negative label.

**Can operating judgment be reused?**  
If a workflow repeats every week, I ask whether it can become a template, SOP, script or small tool.

**Can AI output be verified?**  
I prefer **evidence → insight → action → next validation** over a polished answer with no traceable basis.
